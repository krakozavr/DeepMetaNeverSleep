# DeepMeta Never Sleep

**DeepMeta Never Sleep** is a free, independent browser extension for contributors working on [DeepMeta](https://deepmeta.creativ.zone/) (creativ.zone). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by DeepMeta, creativ.zone, or Google.

## What it does

- **Prevents your computer from sleeping** while a DeepMeta file upload is in progress, so long uploads don't get interrupted.
- **Restores your search results and scroll position** when you return to a batch list after opening a brief.
- **Optional Google Tasks integration** — when you connect your Google account, the extension can create a task in your own Google Tasks account whenever you like a brief on DeepMeta, as a reminder.

The extension runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer — see the Privacy Policy below for details.
